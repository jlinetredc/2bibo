# PARENT-SAFETY.md

## Parent Area goals

Parents should be able to:

- choose/manage profiles
- set play-time reminders
- enable/disable games
- enable Quiet Mode
- review simple activity summaries

## Parent Gate

Default:
- press and hold for about 3 seconds

Provide accessible alternative.

Do not use child private information as a password.

## Play-time options

Suggested:

```text
15 minutes
30 minutes
45 minutes
No limit
```

Behavior:
- gentle reminder
- warning before target
- do not abruptly terminate an active interaction

## Activity summary

Allowed:

```text
Today
Blocks    8 min
Puzzle    5 min
Maze      7 min
```

May also show:
- recently played games
- favorites
- local completion/progress

Do not display:
- IQ
- intelligence ranking
- “smart / weak”
- diagnosis
- pseudo-medical assessment

## Privacy

V1 is local-first.

Do not request:
- exact birth date
- address
- school
- child email
- child phone
- precise geolocation

## Ads and commerce

No child-facing ads.

No rewarded ads.

No loot boxes.

No purchases inside child gameplay.

## Safety of content

Avoid:
- frightening imagery
- violence as the central play loop
- gambling mechanics
- manipulative scarcity
- dark patterns

## Parent game permissions

Parent may toggle individual games.

Disabled games should be hidden or clearly unavailable without enticing child-facing purchase prompts.
